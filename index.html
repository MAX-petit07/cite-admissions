Who can apply

CIUP accepts four kinds of profile. This is a summary of CIUP's own conditions, not a decision about your case.

  • Students and top-level athletes

    • Enrolled in a Master's or doctorate (Bac+4 or equivalent) at a French public or private higher-education institution, preferably in Île-de-France.
    • Arts students can apply once their programme has reached 3 years of higher education at a recognised institution.
    • Athletes must also be enrolled in higher education.

  • Researchers

    • Hold a doctorate and have a post-doctoral project, or be a teacher-researcher.
    • The project is accepted by, or follows an invitation from, a higher-education or research institution (Île-de-France).

  • Artists

    • Have finished their studies, with a specific artistic project in Île-de-France (for example an exhibition or a performance).
    • This includes cultural professionals: curators, archivists, librarians and technicians.

  • Passing guests

    • Exceptionally, people with an academic or cultural link to a house.
    • Maximum stay: 1 month.

Some houses have additional conditions on their own pages (see the Maisons). Source: ciup.fr, checked 10 November 2026

How to apply

There are three ways to apply. Which one is yours depends on your school or your nationality.

  1. 1

    School house

    For students of some schools, for example AgroParisTech or Arts et Métiers.

    Contact: your school's house only.

  2. 2

    National house

    For nationalities represented by a house. The house is your point of contact, and admission then covers the whole Cité.

    Contact: the house for your country only.

  3. 3

    Central admissions service

    For everyone else. Online only.

    Email (students and athletes): admissions.etudiants@ciup.fr

    Researchers: admissions.chercheurs@ciup.fr · Artists: admissions.artistes@ciup.fr

Key facts from CIUP

  • No deadline

    New residents are accepted all year.

  • April

    For a September arrival, apply in April.

    The French and English pages differ slightly, so check with CIUP.

  • 15 days

    No offer 15 days before your arrival date means your application wasn't accepted.

  • 2 things

    You need a copy of your passport or ID card and an ID photo to start.

The answer comes by email, whatever the outcome. CIUP publishes no general processing time.

Source: ciup.fr, checked 10 November 2026

What if I'm not accepted?

Official

If you have no accommodation offer 15 days before your desired arrival date, your application has not been accepted.
