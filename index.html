Key facts from CIUP

  • No deadline

    New residents are accepted all year.

  • April

    For a September arrival, apply in April.

    The French and English pages differ slightly, so check with CIUP.

  • 15 days

    No offer 15 days before your arrival date means your application wasn't accepted.

  • 2 things

    You need a copy of your passport or ID card and an ID photo to start.

Source: ciup.fr, checked 10 November 2026

What if I'm not accepted?

Official

If you have no accommodation offer 15 days before your desired arrival date, your application has not been accepted.
