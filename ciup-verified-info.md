# CIUP — Verified information for Cité Admissions

Collected from the official website www.ciup.fr on **10 November 2026**.
Only facts stated on official CIUP pages are included. Anything not found is listed under "Not found on the official site". Re-check before the final presentation, because the site can change.

---

## 1. Who can apply

Source: https://www.ciup.fr/en/apply-for-accommodation/ (French: https://www.ciup.fr/demander-hebergement/)

| Profile | Conditions (as stated by CIUP) |
|---|---|
| **Students & top-level athletes** | Enrolled in a **Master's or doctorate (Bac+4 or equivalent)** at a French public or private higher-education institution, **preferably in Île-de-France**. Arts students can apply once their programme has reached 3 years of higher education at a recognised institution. Athletes must also be enrolled in higher education. |
| **Researchers** | Hold a doctorate and have a post-doctoral project, or be a teacher-researcher. Project accepted by, or invitation from, a higher-education or research institution (Île-de-France). |
| **Artists** | Finished their studies, with a specific artistic project in Île-de-France (exhibition, performance…). Includes cultural professionals (curators, archivists, librarians, technicians). |
| **Passing guests** | Exceptionally, people with an academic or cultural link to a house. **Maximum stay: 1 month.** |

Some houses have **additional conditions** listed on their own pages.

## 2. The three application routes

| Route | Who | Point of contact |
|---|---|---|
| **School house** (Maison d'école) | e.g. agronomy (AgroParisTech) or Arts et Métiers students | That house only |
| **National house** (Maison nationale) | Nationalities represented by a house | That house only. Admission then covers the whole Cité. |
| **Central admissions service** | Everyone else | Online form + email (below) |

**Admissions emails**
- Students / athletes: admissions.etudiants@ciup.fr
- Researchers: admissions.chercheurs@ciup.fr
- Artists: admissions.artistes@ciup.fr

## 3. How to apply

1. Prepare **a copy of your passport or ID card** and **an ID photo**.
2. Fill in the online form for your profile: https://bienvenue.ciup.fr/questionnaire/
3. Create a username and password (to follow your request), then click **"send"**.
4. You should get an **automatic acknowledgement email**. If you don't, submit again.
5. Your request is reviewed by **admissions committees that meet several times a year**.

Applications are **online only**.

## 4. When to apply

- **No deadline**: new residents are accepted throughout the academic year.
- **For a September arrival**: the French page says apply **from April** of that year ("à partir d'avril"). The English page says requests must be **completed in April**. ⚠️ The two versions differ, so check with CIUP before giving students a firm instruction.

## 5. Getting an answer

- The result is sent **by email** to the address in your application, whatever the outcome.
- **If you have no accommodation offer 15 days before your desired arrival date, your application has not been accepted.**
- No general processing time is published.

➡️ For the prototype: this "15 days before arrival" rule is the only official timing rule. It's the right basis for the "Receive a response" step and for the Plan B feature.

## 6. Documents

Officially listed before starting the form:
- Copy of an official ID (passport or ID card)
- ID photo

No other documents are listed on the application page. Individual houses may ask for more.

## 7. Key numbers (as stated by CIUP)

- About **7,000–7,200 accommodations**: studios, single or double rooms, and 3- to 5-room apartments
- About **12,000 residents** of **150 nationalities** per year
- **47 houses** in a 34-hectare park

⚠️ The house count differs between pages: 43 (meta description), 47 (body text), and 48 entries in the list below.

## 8. Support for residents: acc&ss

Source: https://www.ciup.fr/services/access/
CIUP's support service for international **residents** (students, PhD candidates, researchers). It helps with visas, residence permits, work authorisation, health insurance, housing-aid applications, transport, banking and phone services.
Location: Maison internationale, 21 boulevard Jourdan, 75014 Paris. Hours and contact: https://portail.ciup.fr/services/access-aide-et-accompagnement
