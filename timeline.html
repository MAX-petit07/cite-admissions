Step 1 of 4

Can I apply?

See if one of CIUP's four profiles fits you.

In shortStudents apply from a Master's or doctorate (Bac+4 or equivalent). Researchers, artists and passing guests have their own conditions. This is a summary of CIUP's own rules, not a decision about your case.

For students: the basics

Enrolled in a Master's or doctorate (Bac+4 or equivalent) at a French public or private higher-education institution, preferably in Île-de-France.

  • Students and top-level athletes

    Enrolled in a Master's or doctorate, as above.

    Read more
    • Arts students can apply once their programme has reached 3 years of higher education at a recognised institution.
    • Athletes must also be enrolled in higher education.

  • Researchers

    A doctorate and a post-doctoral project, or a teacher-researcher.

    Read more
    • The project is accepted by, or follows an invitation from, a higher-education or research institution (Île-de-France).

  • Artists

    Studies finished, with a specific artistic project in Île-de-France.

    Read more
    • For example an exhibition or a performance.
    • This includes cultural professionals: curators, archivists, librarians and technicians.

  • Passing guests

    Exceptionally, people with an academic or cultural link to a house. Maximum stay: 1 month.

Some houses have extra conditions on their own pages (see Houses). Source: ciup.fr, checked 10 November 2026.

Step 2 of 4

Choose my route

Find out who to contact: your school, your country's house, or CIUP.

In shortThere are three ways to apply. Yours depends on your school or your nationality. A Maison (house) is one of the residences listed on the CIUP website.

Your Plan A

  1. School house (Maison d'école)

    For students of some schools, for example AgroParisTech or Arts et Métiers.

    Contact: your school's house only.

  2. National house (Maison nationale)

    For nationalities represented by a house. The house is your point of contact, and admission then covers the whole Cité.

    Contact: the house for your country only.

  3. Central admissions service

    For everyone else. Online only.

    Students and athletes: admissions.etudiants@ciup.fr

    Other emails
    • Researchers: admissions.chercheurs@ciup.fr
    • Artists: admissions.artistes@ciup.fr
    Official online form

Why might I get a different house?

After you are admitted, the house director can ask you to live in your country's or school's house, or in another house. CIUP calls this “brassage” (mixing): it helps residents of all nationalities meet.

Source: CIUP admission regulation (PDF, in French), checked 24 November 2026.

FAQ

Quick answers

Short answers, only from official CIUP information.

Is MANI official?

No. MANI is an independent student project and is not affiliated with the Cité Internationale Universitaire de Paris. Always check the official CIUP website.

Is there a deadline?

No. New residents are accepted throughout the academic year. For a September arrival, apply in April. The French and English CIUP pages word this slightly differently, so check with CIUP.

How do I apply?

Online only. Your route depends on your school or your nationality: a school house, a national house, or the central admissions service. Step 2 explains the three routes.

When will I get an answer?

By email, whatever the outcome. Admissions committees meet several times a year, and CIUP publishes no general processing time.

What if I have no offer 15 days before I arrive?

Then your application has not been accepted. The English and French CIUP pages word this rule differently, so ask the admissions service if you are unsure. Step 4 has our Plan B tips.
