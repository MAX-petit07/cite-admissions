Become an alumni volunteer

Lived at the Cité? Share your experience with students who are about to apply. You choose what is shown, and you answer when you have time.

Community rules

For everyone: students and alumni.

  • Be kind and respectful. Everyone was new once.

  • Alumni share experience, not official decisions. Always check ciup.fr.

  • Never send money to anyone you meet here.

  • Never share your passport, ID or application documents with an alumni.

  • No room sales, sublets or "guaranteed places". Admission only happens through CIUP's official channels.

  • Keep personal contact details private until you're comfortable.

  • Report anything suspicious: Report a problem (placeholder contact, to be replaced by our team's real address)

Help

Can an alumni help me get accepted?

No. Admission is decided by CIUP. Alumni can only share their experience.

Is this an official CIUP service?

No, it's a student prototype, not affiliated with CIUP.

How fast will they answer?

Alumni are volunteers, so replies can take a few days.

What if I have an official question?

Contact CIUP: admissions.etudiants@ciup.fr or www.ciup.fr/contact.
