Community rules

For everyone who asks or answers.

  • Be kind and respectful. Everyone was new once.

  • Answers here are personal experience or MANI's own checks, never official CIUP decisions. Always check ciup.fr.

  • Never send money to anyone you meet here.

  • Never share your passport, ID or application documents with anyone you meet here.

  • No room sales, sublets or "guaranteed places". Admission only happens through CIUP's official channels.

  • Keep personal contact details private until you're comfortable.

  • Report anything suspicious: Report a problem (each question and answer also has a Report link)

Help

Can someone here help me get accepted?

No. Admission is decided by CIUP. People here can only share what they know or what they experienced.

Is this an official CIUP service?

No. MANI is an independent student project and is not affiliated with CIUP.

What does “Checked by the MANI team” mean?

A member of the MANI team compared the answer with the source it cites. It does not mean CIUP has approved it. Always check the cited source yourself.

What does “Alumni — unverified” mean?

The person says they lived at the Cité, but MANI has not verified it yet. “Verified alumni” means a MANI team member verified it.

Are new posts checked before they appear?

No. Posts appear immediately. The MANI team can hide a post that breaks the community rules. See something wrong? Use the Report link on any question or answer.

What if I have an official question?

Contact CIUP: admissions.etudiants@ciup.fr or www.ciup.fr/contact.
